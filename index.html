Quant Planet

Short educational videos about trading math, risk and statistics.

Quant Planet (also called Quant Theory) is a faceless educational channel. Each video uses animated charts built from real market data or clearly labeled simulations to explain one idea: win rate versus risk, losing streaks, position sizing, fat tails and similar topics. Nothing here is financial advice.
