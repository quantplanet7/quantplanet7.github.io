Privacy Policy: Quant Planet (Instagram)

Effective October 25, 2026

Quant Planet is a private tool used by the owner of the Instagram account @quantplanet7 to manage that one account. It is not offered to the public, and the account owner is its only user.

What it accesses

When the account owner signs in with Instagram, the tool uses the Instagram API to:

  • read the account's own profile, posts and insights (views, likes, saves, watch time), to see how the videos perform;
  • publish the account's own videos (Reels) at scheduled times;
  • read comments left on the account's own posts, so the owner can see viewers' questions and answer them.

It does not access any other account's posts, messages or followers, and it does not collect data about people who comment beyond what the comment shows on the owner's own post (the comment text, username and time).

How the data is used and stored

  • The sign-in token is stored only on the account owner's own computer. It is sent only to Meta's own servers when the tool talks to the Instagram API.
  • Statistics and comments are saved to files on that same computer, to plan future videos and reply to questions.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Data deletion

To remove the tool's access, open Instagram → Settings → Website permissions (or Apps and websites) → Active → Quant Planet → Remove. The owner deletes the saved token, statistics and comments from the computer at any time.

If you commented on one of our posts and want any saved copy of your comment removed, email quantplanet7@gmail.com with your username. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm. Deleting your comment on Instagram also removes it from future reads.
