Privacy Policy: quantvideouploader

Effective October 22, 2026

quantvideouploader is a private tool used by the owner of the Quant Planet channel to publish the channel's own videos to its own YouTube account. It is not offered to the public, and the channel owner is its only user.

What it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for permission to:

  • upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel (youtube.upload);
  • view the channel's own video list and details (youtube.readonly), to avoid uploading a video twice;
  • view the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly), to see how the videos perform.

It cannot comment, edit or delete videos, and it does not access any other Google data.

How the data is used and stored

  • The sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. It is not sent anywhere except to Google's own servers when the tool talks to the YouTube APIs.
  • View counts and similar statistics are saved to files on that same computer, to plan future videos.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's own handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The saved token and statistics can be deleted from the owner's computer at any time.
